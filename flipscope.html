What FlipScope is

A free deal analyzer for fix-and-flip investors. Load a property's photos, click through them while you build a line-by-line repair scope with costs adjusted for the local market, then pull it all into an ARV / max-allowable-offer calculation with buy-side closing, holding, selling costs and your target profit.

Everything runs in your browser. Photos, numbers and saved deals stay on your device (nothing is uploaded). Cost presets are calibrated for Utah and adjustable everywhere — plug in your own trade bids as you get them.

Built by Lee Schoettle, Utah wholesaler & flipper.

5 After-Repair Value (ARV)

What the house sells for fixed up. Pull recent sold comps, then set the number.
Open sold comps near this property:
Pick 3–5 recently sold, updated homes of similar size/beds within ~½ mile. Median $/sqft × this home's sqft ≈ ARV. Trim for a smaller lot, busy street, or dated street appeal.
