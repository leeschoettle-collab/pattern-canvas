PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Lee Schoettle Realty (Lee Schoettle, LS Realty, "we") collects and uses information through leeschoettle.com and our phone and text line.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us in forms, by email, phone or text: your name, phone number, email address, property address and details about your property or goals.
  • Basic website usage data collected by Google Analytics, such as pages visited and general location.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, evaluate your property and make offers.
  • To schedule calls and appointments with you.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive.

Text messaging

If you opt in, Lee Schoettle Realty may text you about your inquiry and appointment scheduling. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver messages on our behalf.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example our form, phone, texting and scheduling providers), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us below.
